# Corrections and interpretive notes

This file exists because the build spec treats hand review of generated
criteria trees as the highest-value manual step in the project, and asks for
corrections to be recorded rather than hidden. In practice, the eight trees in
this directory were **authored directly against the fetched source text**
(`pipeline/build_policies.py`), not generated by an LLM and then corrected —
`pipeline/parse_policy.py` implements the generate-then-review path from the
spec and will write to `data/policies/generated/` for comparison, but running
it requires `OPENROUTER_API_KEY`, which was not available in this build
environment. Anyone with a key can run:

```
python3 pipeline/parse_policy.py --all
python3 pipeline/parse_policy.py --diff
```

and any disagreement it surfaces should be added below, whichever side turns
out to be wrong.

What follows instead is the record of the interpretive decisions and one
genuine encoding workaround made while writing the trees by hand.

## home_oxygen — "greater than 56%" has no strict-inequality operator

Source (Group II, secondary findings): *"Erythrocythemia with a hematocrit
greater than 56%."*

The predicate grammar in `pipeline/models.py` only offers `>=`, `<=`, `==`,
`!=`, `includes`, `excludes` — no strict `>`. Hematocrit is conventionally
reported to one decimal place, so the leaf is encoded as
`hematocrit_percent >= 56.1` rather than `> 56`. This is functionally
equivalent for any value reported to that precision and is *not* equivalent
at higher precision (e.g. 56.05 would be excluded here but included by a
literal `> 56`). Noted rather than silently rounded.

## home_oxygen — the exercise-desaturation qualifier attaches to one branch only

Source: *"An arterial PO2 at or below 55 mm Hg ... taken during exercise ...
for a patient who demonstrates an arterial PO2 at or above 56 mm Hg ... during
the day while at rest."*

This is exactly the scope trap the build spec calls out: a duration/threshold
qualifier that attaches to one modality (exercise) and not its siblings
(at-rest, during-sleep). The tree encodes it as `qualifying_on_exercise = AND(
exercise_desaturation, sat_rest_adequate )`, a sibling of — not a shared parent
over — the at-rest and during-sleep branches. Getting this wrong (hanging the
"adequate at rest" leaf off the top-level OR) would incorrectly require every
Group I patient, including those who desaturate at rest, to also prove they
are fine at rest.

## home_oxygen — "or" inside an "and" list

Same NCD, Group II: the qualifying PO2/saturation band is itself an "or" over
two measurements (PO2 band, or saturation exactly 89%), then joined by "and"
to "at least one of" three secondary findings. Encoded as
`group_ii = AND(borderline_hypoxemia, secondary_finding)`, with
`borderline_hypoxemia = OR(po2_band, sat_89)` and `secondary_finding = N_OF(1,
...)`. A flat AND-of-five-leaves reading would have silently required all
three secondary findings simultaneously.

## bariatric_surgery — "at least one co-morbidity" is N_OF(1), not AND

Source: *"have at least one co-morbidity related to obesity."* Encoded as
`N_OF(1, comorbid_diabetes, comorbid_sleep_apnea, comorbid_hypertension)`
rather than OR, to match the spec's guidance that counted alternatives should
use N_OF. Functionally identical to OR here (n=1 over any list behaves like
OR) but keeps the encoding consistent with the two- and three-of-N clauses
elsewhere in the corpus, and reads correctly if a reviewer ever changes the
threshold.

## pet_oncology — "three FDG PET scans are nationally covered ... more than
three ... at MAC discretion"

Encoded as `prior_subsequent_pet_scans <= 2` (i.e., this would be at most the
third study) rather than `<= 3`, because the predicate is evaluated *before*
the study being requested, not counting it. A fourth-or-later study is MAC
discretionary, not nationally covered, so it correctly falls outside the tree
the moment the prior count reaches 3.

## tavr — two independent volume-qualification routes are an OR, not an AND

Source describes "qualifications to begin a TAVR program for hospitals
without TAVR experience" as one path and implies an established-program path
as the other. Encoded as
`volume_requirements = OR(existing_program, new_program_volumes)`. Reading
the two qualification sets as cumulative (AND) would make it impossible for
any hospital — new or established — to ever qualify, since an established
program by definition no longer has to prove the *new*-program volumes.

## lumbar_fusion, cgm — LCD paraphrase caveat

Both trees are **paraphrases**, not transcriptions: CMS returns HTTP 401 for
LCD detail text (`/v1/data/lcd`) without a licence token — verified directly
against `api.coverage.cms.gov`, not assumed — so the specific thresholds
(6 months of conservative therapy, 6-month visit recency, etc.) could not be
checked against the published document. Both are marked
`"verification": "unverified-paraphrase"` in their JSON and flagged in the UI
with a warning badge. They are included to demonstrate the nesting patterns
LCDs typically use, not as authoritative statements of L37848 or L33822's
actual criteria.
