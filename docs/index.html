    All 20 cases are synthetic. They were written for this project to mirror the style of discharge and consult notes. They are not real patient records and not drawn from any credentialed corpus.

    The engine determines whether the documented facts satisfy published coverage criteria. It does not model payer behaviour, adjudication practice, or the probability of denial.

    Confidence is rule-derived from the tree, not calibrated against payer outcomes. Two of the eight policies are paraphrases of licence-gated LCDs and are labelled as unverified.
